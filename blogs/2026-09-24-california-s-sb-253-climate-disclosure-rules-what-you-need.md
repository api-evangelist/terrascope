Summary Senate Bill 253 requires companies with revenue above USD 1 billion that do business in California to disclose Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions annually to CARB, measured under the Greenhouse Gas Protocol and covered by third-party assurance. The first Scope 1 and 2 reports are due on 10 November 2026, with Scope 3 reporting starting in 2027 on the schedule CARB sets in its second rulemaking.
