EXECUTIVE SUMMARY Climate impact from avoided emissions : Winning Group quantified the climate impact of its longstanding removal and recycling program for the first time, revealing ~ 8.2 kilotons of CO2e avoided. Scope 3 hotspots: Winning Group also measured their carbon footprint for FY21-FY25 and identified their emission hot spots . Scope 3 accounted for a significant proportion, with use of sold products and purchased goods & services representing material emissions sources.
