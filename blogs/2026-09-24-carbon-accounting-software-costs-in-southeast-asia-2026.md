Summary Publicly listed prices for carbon accounting software in Southeast Asia fall into three bands: free Scope 1 and 2 tools, mid-market platforms at about USD 33,000 to 88,000 a year, and enterprise platforms at about USD 55,000 to 260,000+ a year. Most vendors publish no prices. The figures here come from cloud marketplace listings and procurement data, checked in September 2026.
